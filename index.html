    Michael Kofman is a technology executive and company founder. His work spans satellite engineering, internet infrastructure, public technology companies, and life sciences. He founded 9 Net Avenue Inc., acquired by Concentric Networks in 2000. He is CEO of Digital Invest Inc., in bio-mathematical medicine and genomic intelligence, and founder of AGRON Inc., in autonomous aerial-ground operations and geospatial systems. He serves on boards and advises in technology, health, and infrastructure.
